PVD and heart risk check

Estimates the risk of peripheral vascular disease (PVD) from routine health measures. If PVD is likely, a second model estimates cardiovascular risk and shows how much PVD adds to it.

  • 4 model types to compare
  • Built from 70,000 patient records
  • Runs in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere

How it works

Stage one screens for PVD from 11 routine measures. If PVD is likely, stage two estimates cardiovascular risk and compares population risk with and without PVD.

Private by design

The trained models were converted from Python to JavaScript and run on your device. Your answers are never uploaded or stored.
